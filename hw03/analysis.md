# HW3 Analysis: Do Executive Changes Precede or Follow Earnings?

Executive changes in this data mostly come before earnings announcements. The timeline counts 21 before, 9 after and 2 in the same week; without the 4 compensation-only 5.02 filings, that is 20 before, 6 after and 2 same week out of 28 real events. The pattern is driven by the major successions, which were disclosed in the quiet weeks ahead of results: Apple's Cook-to-Ternus CEO handover (10 days before earnings), JPMorgan's Co-President promotions with Marianne Lake's retirement (19 days before) and Walmart's McMillon-to-Furner CEO succession (6 days before, same week). It varies by company, though: Microsoft's two events (a director joining and one leaving the board) both came after earnings, and NVIDIA was mixed. The caveat is a small sample (five companies, one year), and an 8-K filing date marks when a change was disclosed, not when it was decided.
